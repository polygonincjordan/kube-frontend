Low and High Fall Risk Prevention Protocol

High Fall Risk Prevention Protocol
  1. 1.Follow pediatrics' low fall risk preventive intervention/None
  2. 2.Consider moving patients closer to nurses' station
  3. 3.Assist patients and provide staff with ambulation
  4. 4.Check patient minimum every 1 hour
  5. 5.Assessment and follow up of patient after receiving drug-induced fall by the order of a physician
  6. 6.Place fall prevention sign on the patient's room door frame and on the patient's cover document
Low and High Fall Risk Prevention Protocol
  1. 1.Nurse call within reach, educate patients and families on its functionality
  2. 2.Oriented patients and family about the environment and organized the environment safe from hazards. Place the furniture in the right position.
  3. 3.Keep beds in low position with brakes on and bed ends in place.
  4. 4.Side rails x 2 or 4 up, assess large gaps, suck that a patient could get extremity or other body part entrapped, use
    additional safety procedures. Use of non-skid footwear for ambulating patients, use of appropriate size clothing to prevent risk tripping.
  5. 5.Assess for adequate lighting, leave nightlight on.
  6. 6.Assess eliminations need, assist as needed
  7. 7.Educate patient/parents of fall protocol precautions
  8. 8.Educating and documentation in medication record about fall preventive information.
  9. 9.Assessment and follow-up of patients after receiving drug-induced fall by the order of a physician.
