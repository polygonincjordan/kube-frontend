Care plan
High risk
  • Take action
  • Refer the child to a Dietitian, nutritional support team, or consultant
  • Monitor as per care plan
Medium risk
  • Monitor the child’s nutritional intake for 3 days
  • Repeat the STAMP screening after 3 days
  • Amend care plan as required
Low risk
  • Continue routine clinical care
  • Repeat the STAMP screening weekly while the child is an in-patient
  • Amend care plan as required
